KEY FEATURES:

• Water Tracking — Log your daily water intake with different drink types

• AI Hydration Coach — AI automatically adjusts your water goal based on weather, activity, sleep

• Smart Reminders — The system knows when you're sleeping, in a meeting, or driving

• Deep Analytics — Weekly and monthly charts with detailed health reports

• Device Integration — Sync with Google Fit and the DigiBottle smart cup via Bluetooth

• Community — Share progress, join clubs, compete on leaderboards

• Security — Biometric authentication, safe cloud data sync

DigiWell helps you build healthy hydration habits.
