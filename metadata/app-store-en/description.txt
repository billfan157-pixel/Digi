DigiWell is a smart water tracking and wellness app, personalized for every user.

KEY FEATURES:

• Water Tracking — Log your daily water intake with different drink types (water, coffee, tea, juice, and more)

• AI Hydration Coach — Artificial intelligence automatically adjusts your daily water goal based on weather, activity, sleep, and personal habits

• Smart Reminders — The notification system knows when you're sleeping, in a meeting, or driving so it never interrupts at the wrong time

• Deep Analytics — Weekly and monthly charts with detailed health reports

• Device Integration — Sync with Apple Health, Google Fit, and the DigiBottle smart cup via Bluetooth

• Community — Share progress, join clubs, compete on leaderboards, and complete quests with friends

• Security — Biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID), safe cloud data sync

DigiWell helps you build healthy hydration habits naturally and scientifically.
