## 3. Description (English)

**DigiWell turns daily hydration into a fun, social, and intelligent habit.**

Tired of boring water trackers? DigiWell combines smart reminders, gamified challenges, AI-powered insights, and social accountability — all in one beautifully designed app.

**✦ Smart Logging**
Log water in seconds with Quick Add. Track any drink type with custom hydration factors. Your data syncs seamlessly across devices.

**✦ Gamified Challenges**
Join Guilds, compete in Arena battles, complete Quests, and earn rewards. Hydration has never been this fun.

**✦ AI-Powered Insights**
Get personalized hydration schedules, smart reminders based on weather & activity, and weekly reports that help you understand your habits.

**✦ Social Accountability**
Create or join Guilds with friends. Clink posts, splash water, send nudges, and challenge each other to stay hydrated.

**✦ Detailed Analytics**
Track your daily intake, view historical trends, monitor streaks, and export your data for medical or fitness use.

**✦ Apple Health & Google Health Connect**
Sync your hydration data with Apple Health or Health Connect for a complete wellness picture.

**✦ Biometric Security**
Protect your data with Face ID or fingerprint unlock.

---
